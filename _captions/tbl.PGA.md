Peak ground acceleration, $\mathrm{PGA}$ [%1$s], for site `%3$s` at $V_{S30}$ = %2$d m/s. $T_R$ is the return period, in years, and $P_T$ is the probability of at least one exceedance during the exposure interval of the calculation, as a percentage. The remaining columns are the demand statistics: those headed by a probability level $p$ hold the quantile $Q(p)$, and the one headed `mean` holds the mean, both in the units stated. The `MCE` row reports the deterministic envelope over the scenarios of each statistic: the envelope of the scenario means in the `mean` column and of the scenario quantiles in the columns headed by $p$; it carries no return period, so $T_R$ and $P_T$ do not apply.
