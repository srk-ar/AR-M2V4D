Peak ground acceleration by return period and site condition (`siteID`: %s). Columns identified by $V_{S30}$ (m/s) represent the site conditions. The $T_R$ rows (years) report the mean probabilistic PGA (%s), and the MDE (84%%) row reports the 0.84 quantile of the PGA for the return period of the MDE. The MCE and MCE (84%%) rows report the deterministic envelope at its mean and 84th percentile, respectively, for the same site conditions.
