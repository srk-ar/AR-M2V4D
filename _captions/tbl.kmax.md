Performance-based seismic coefficients $k_{\max}$ [%1$s] for geometric scenario %2$s and material model %3$s at site `%5$s`. `ID` identifies the seismic demand &mdash;the MCE or a return period $T_R$ of %4$s years&mdash; and `IDn` the set of Newmark models &mdash;`ensemble`, `rigid` or `flexible`&mdash;. $T_s$ is the fundamental period of the slope, in seconds, and $V_{S30}$ the shear-wave velocity of the evaluated condition, in m/s. Each remaining column is headed by an allowable permanent displacement relative to the slope height, $D_a/H_s$ [%%], and holds the arithmetic mean of $k_{\max}$ across the pooled realizations of the models of the set at that limit, the roots $k_{\max}=0$ included, expressed as an acceleration in the stated unit; the fractiles associated with the exceedance target $p_e$ are those defined for the criterion in the seismic-coefficient chapter. Values marked with an asterisk have a $k_{\max}$, in $g$, outside the $k_y$ calibration interval of the model. For a set of models, a mark means that at least one active model of the set is outside its interval at that $k_{\max}$.
