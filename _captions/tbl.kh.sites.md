MCE seismic coefficients $k_h$ [% of PGA] from the `ensemble` set. Each cell reports the minimum–maximum range across the material scenarios, from their mean estimates. The range describes variation between materials; it is not a confidence interval or a simulation fractile. `siteID` identifies the site and $H_s$ the slope height, in m. The remaining column headings are the relative displacement limits $D_a/H_s$ [%]. The column colors match the performance bands in the figures. A material is marked when its $k_{\max}$, in $g$, is positive and lies outside the $k_y$ calibration interval of at least one active model of the `ensemble` set. A material range is marked with an asterisk when at least one contributing material is marked.
