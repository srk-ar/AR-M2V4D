Performance-based seismic coefficients $k_h$ [%% of $\mathrm{PGA}$] for geometric scenario %1$s and material model %2$s at site `%4$s`. `ID` identifies the seismic demand —the MCE or a return period $T_R$ of %3$s years— and `IDn` the set of Newmark models —`ensemble`, `rigid` or `flexible`—. $T_s$ is the fundamental period of the slope, in seconds, and $V_{S30}$ the shear-wave velocity of the evaluated condition, in m/s. Each remaining column is headed by an allowable permanent displacement relative to the slope height, $D_a/H_s$ [%%], and holds the mean estimate of the coefficient $k_h=100\,k_{\max}/\mathrm{PGA}(V_{S30})$, that is, $k_{\max}$ expressed as a percentage of the site-condition $\mathrm{PGA}$. Values marked with an asterisk have a $k_{\max}$, in $g$, outside the $k_y$ calibration interval of the model. For a set of models, a mark means that at least one active model of the set is outside its interval at that $k_{\max}$.
