Site amplification factors $\mathrm{AF}$, dimensionless, of the design demand for site `%7$s` relative to the $V_{S30}=760$ m/s reference condition, for return periods from %1$s to %2$s yr, oscillator periods from %3$g to %4$g s, the site condition $V_{S30}$ = %5$s m/s, and the statistic $p$ = %6$s. When the report covers more than one site, a leading `siteID` column identifies the site. The identifying columns are $T_n$, the oscillator period, in seconds, with $T_n=0$ representing $\mathrm{PGA}$; $V_{S30}$, the shear-wave velocity of the evaluated condition, in m/s; and $p$, the reported statistic, the ensemble mean or the quantile probability level, dimensionless. Each remaining column is headed by a return period $T_R$, in years, and holds the corresponding $\mathrm{AF}$ factor.
