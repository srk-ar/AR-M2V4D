MCE and MDE design spectra (PSHA/DSHA) for site `%2$s` at the reference rock condition $V_{S30} = %1$.0f$ m/s. The MCE is derived from the deterministic scenarios; the MDE is the mean rock UHS for $T_R = 10{,}000$ yr. The ordinate is the spectral acceleration $S_a(T_n)$, in $g$, with 5%% critical damping; the abscissa is the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, with the $T_n = 0$ ordinate labelled $\mathrm{PGA}$. Both axes are logarithmic. For each product, the thick solid line is the mean spectrum and the shaded region spans from that mean to the 0.84 quantile of the same demand, bounded by thin lines.
