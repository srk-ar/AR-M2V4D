Site amplification factors $\mathrm{AF}$, dimensionless, of the design demand for site `%3$s` relative to the 760 m/s reference rock condition, as a function of the oscillator period $T_n$, in seconds, whose value $T_n=0$ is labelled $\mathrm{PGA}$. Each series corresponds to a combination of hazard scenario `ID`, return period $T_R$, and probability level $p$: the `mean` level is the mean of the amplification ratio and the numerical levels are its quantiles $Q(p)$, whose extremes delimit the total variability. $V_{S30} = %1$s$ m/s and $T_R = %2$s$ yr.
