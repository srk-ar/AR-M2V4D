Cross section of the slope as a truncated shear wedge, with slope height $H_s$ from the crest to the base, berm width $b$, base width $b_{\max}$ and slope ratio $s$, the horizontal advance of each slope per unit of height. The extended slopes, dashed above the crest, meet at the apex: $H=H_s/(1-\lambda_o)$ is the height from the apex to the base, $h=H-H_s=\lambda_o H$ the height of the apex above the berm, and $\lambda_o=b/b_{\max}$ the truncation ratio.
