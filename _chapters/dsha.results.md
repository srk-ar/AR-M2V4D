The results of the deterministic assessment correspond to the maximum horizontal component of $S_a(T_n)$ at 5% of critical damping on reference rock, $V_{S30}=760$ m/s. The mean is presented for each scenario; the MCE and the MCE (84%) are obtained through independent per-period maxima. The table presents the spectral ordinates of the scenarios and their MCE envelopes; the graphical superposition of those spectra and the available mean uniform-hazard spectra is provided in the online results identified at the beginning of this section. The governing scenario is identified separately for each period and statistic, and that identification represents the changes of controlling source along the spectrum, so each envelope preserves the identity of the scenario contributing its ordinate at each period.
